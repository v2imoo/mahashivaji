The second qualification

In 2023, more than ten lakh candidates applied for 4,644 posts of Talathi, among them engineers, MBAs and doctorates. A strong second path is prudence, not retreat.

Fix the horizon

Decide the number of attempts before you begin, and hold to it.

Build in parallel

Earn a second, marketable qualification from the pathways above while you prepare.
