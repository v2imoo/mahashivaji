// Eight frontier sectors

Where opportunity is moving.

For each: why it matters, the skills it rewards, and where to begin today.

SECTOR 01

Artificial intelligence & data

Every industry in the state will be reshaped by software that learns. The advantage goes to those who can apply it, not merely use it.

Skills
Applied AI, data analysis, AI governance
Begin
Elements of AI, then NPTEL machine learning
SECTOR 02

Semiconductors & electronics

India is building a chip and electronics manufacturing base. Design, testing and assembly will need skilled people at every level.

Skills
VLSI design, embedded systems, electronics assembly
Begin
India Semiconductor Mission and NPTEL electronics
SECTOR 03

Electric mobility & clean energy

Pune and its surrounding belt are already automotive country. The shift to electric and solar opens work in installation, service and design.

Skills
Solar installation, battery systems, EV servicing
Begin
Solar technician training through MCED
SECTOR 04

Agri-tech & food processing

Maharashtra's farms can capture far more value through processing, cold chains, traceability and direct markets.

Skills
Post-harvest processing, producer companies, digital markets
Begin
e-NAM and your district Krishi Vigyan Kendra
SECTOR 05

Health & life sciences

Diagnostics, medical devices, biotechnology and community health will demand both scientists and trained technicians.

Skills
Lab technology, biotech research, health operations
Begin
Startup support through BIRAC
SECTOR 06

Advanced manufacturing & robotics

Factories are automating. The new work is in programming, maintaining and improving the machines.

Skills
CNC and robotics, quality systems, industrial automation
Begin
Apprenticeships via the National Apprenticeship Portal
SECTOR 07

Space, drones & aerospace

Private space and drone industries are opening in India, from components to data services built on satellite imagery.

Skills
Drone operation, geospatial analysis, precision components
Begin
IN-SPACe for the space ecosystem
SECTOR 08

Creative & digital economy

Marathi cinema, music, design, gaming and content now reach global audiences directly. Craft plus distribution is a career.

Skills
Design, production, digital distribution
Begin
A portfolio, a mentor from the Network, and the Archive
