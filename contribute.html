// Forms of contribution

Five ways to serve.

I

Mentor

Chartered accountants, advocates, engineers, doctors, founders, teachers, farmers and master craftspeople. One hour a month. Credentials verified first.

II

Civic Steward

One trusted person in each pilot area, verifying local records before publication. Training provided.

III

Editorial Reviewer

Review a page in your field before it is published, and record what is inaccurate or missing.

IV

Proposer

Propose a course, a resource, or a person from your district whose work deserves recognition.

V

Partner institution

Colleges, companies, professional firms and diaspora associations that lend mentors, venues and opportunities.

// How to apply

Write to us. We reply within a week.

Choose the subject and your email opens with the details we need already listed. The Foundation never asks for money, from anyone.
