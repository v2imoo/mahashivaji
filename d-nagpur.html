What this file will hold.

Compiled in the open by people from the district. Every line needs a source, and nothing is published until a steward has checked it.

  1. People Population and how it is changing; languages spoken; main livelihoods; migration in and out; the young. Being compiled
  2. Problems What residents themselves report, with evidence: water, roads, jobs, schools, health, credit. Each item dated and sourced. Being compiled
  3. Possibilities Industries, crops, crafts, institutions and natural assets the district can build on, with the people already doing it. Being compiled
  4. Builders Documented stories of people from the district who started with little and built something lasting. Being compiled
  5. Heritage Forts, sites and history, from sources. Being compiled
  6. Doors The offices, portals and programmes that actually serve this district. Being compiled
