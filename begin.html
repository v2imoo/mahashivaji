// Self-inspiration

A practice, not a mood.

Inspiration fades by evening. Practice does not. Three things to return to.

The Builder's Pledge

  1. I will connect: seek out those who know more than I do, and make room for those who know less.
  2. I will create: begin before I feel ready, and finish what I begin.
  3. I will develop: learn something useful every month, for as long as I live.
  4. I will contribute: give an hour each month to someone building their way.
  5. I will improve: fix one thing near me, with evidence, not anger.
  6. I will progress: measure my year honestly, and begin the next one higher.
  7. I will stand against no one: caste, faith, region and party stay outside the work.

Six questions for the evening

  1. Whom did I connect with, or help to connect, today?
  2. What did I make, or move forward?
  3. What did I learn that I did not know this morning?
  4. Whom did I help, without being asked?
  5. What did I fix, however small?
  6. Am I further along than I was yesterday?

The weekly review

  • What did I build this week?
  • Whom did I help, and who helped me?
  • What will I fix next week?
  • Which resolution am I keeping, and which have I let slip?

When the road is hard, speak to someone. Tele-MANAS, the national mental health line, is on 14416.

    // Self-resolve

    Solve it yourself, step by step.

    Plain instructions for the problems people most often wait on others to fix. Rules change; the official source is always the final word.

    Documents and services

    Get a government certificateOnline · fee depends on the service
    1. Register on Aaple Sarkar with your mobile number.
    2. Choose the department and the certificate you need, such as income or domicile.
    3. Upload the documents listed for that service, and pay the stated fee online if there is one.
    4. Note your application number and track it on the portal.
    5. If it is not delivered within the notified time limit, file a first appeal on the same portal.

    Official source: Aaple Sarkar

    Keep your documents safe and shareableOnline · without charge
    1. Sign up on DigiLocker with your mobile number and Aadhaar.
    2. Pull the documents issued to you, such as marksheets or a driving licence, where available.
    3. Share documents from DigiLocker instead of handing over photocopies.

    Official source: DigiLocker

    Find and apply for a scholarshipOnline · without charge
    1. Read the eligibility for central scholarships on the National Scholarship Portal, and for state scholarships on MahaDBT.
    2. Register on the portal that applies to you, and complete your profile carefully.
    3. Keep your bank account details in order, as the portal requires.
    4. Apply before the deadline and save the acknowledgement.

    Official source: National Scholarship Portal

    Check or correct your voter registrationOnline · without charge
    1. Search for your name on the voter services portal.
    2. If you are not registered, apply with Form 6.
    3. To correct details or record a change of address, use Form 8.

    Official source: Election Commission voter services

    Enterprise

    Register your business on UdyamOnline · without charge · about 15 minutes
    1. Keep your Aadhaar and PAN ready.
    2. Open udyamregistration.gov.in, and only this address.
    3. Verify your Aadhaar with the OTP, then enter your PAN and business details.
    4. Submit. Your certificate is sent by email. No agent and no fee are needed.

    Official source: Udyam Registration

    Civic

    File a grievance with the stateOnline · without charge
    1. Register on the state grievance portal.
    2. Select the department responsible.
    3. State the facts, dates and places plainly, and attach documents.
    4. Save the token number and follow up on the portal.

    Official source: Maharashtra grievance portal

    Ask for information under RTIOnline for Mantralaya departments · nominal fee
    1. Frame short, specific questions about records, not opinions.
    2. Apply online to Mantralaya departments; for local offices, apply in writing to the Public Information Officer.
    3. Pay the application fee; applicants below the poverty line are exempt with proof.
    4. A reply is due within thirty days. If none comes, file a first appeal.

    Official source: RTI online, Maharashtra

    See your village's plans and spendingOnline · without charge
    1. Open eGramSwaraj and choose your state, district, block and panchayat.
    2. Read the development plan and the expenditure recorded against it.
    3. Take your questions to the next gram sabha.

    Official source: eGramSwaraj

    Protection

    Report online or UPI fraudAct within minutes · helpline 1930
    1. Call 1930 at once, or report on cybercrime.gov.in.
    2. Ask your bank to block the card or UPI account involved.
    3. Keep screenshots, transaction IDs and phone numbers as evidence.

    Official source: National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

    Make a consumer complaintHelpline 1915 · then online filing
    1. Write to the seller first, and keep the reply.
    2. Register the complaint with the National Consumer Helpline on 1915 or online.
    3. If it remains unresolved, file before the consumer commission online through e-Daakhil.

    Official source: National Consumer Helpline
