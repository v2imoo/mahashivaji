// The one rule
Maharashtra belongs to no caste, no faith and no party. It belongs to those who build it.
We never ask your caste, religion, party or income. Not on a form, not in a circle, not in a conversation.

// Frontier
The industries that will shape the next fifty years.
We watch where the work is moving and tell one another early.
SECTOR 01
Artificial intelligence & data
Every industry in the state will be reshaped by software that learns. The advantage goes to those who can apply it, not merely use it.
SECTOR 02
Semiconductors & electronics
India is building a chip and electronics manufacturing base. Design, testing and assembly will need skilled people at every level.
SECTOR 03
Electric mobility & clean energy
Pune and its surrounding belt are already automotive country. The shift to electric and solar opens work in installation, service and design.
SECTOR 04
Agri-tech & food processing
Maharashtra's farms can capture far more value through processing, cold chains, traceability and direct markets.

// The name
A way of building, not a monument.
Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj protected the cultivator's livelihood, appointed people on merit, drew talent from every quarter, governed in his own language, and prepared for the future before it arrived.
We take the name for that method. It belongs to no party, community or ritual, and we will not let it be used for one.
Six values, each with a source →


// The pledge
Seven lines you say to yourself.
Not to us. A pledge is kept by the person who makes it.
Turn a line into a resolution →
  1. I will connect: seek out those who know more than I do, and make room for those who know less.
  2. I will create: begin before I feel ready, and finish what I begin.
  3. I will develop: learn something useful every month, for as long as I live.
  4. I will contribute: give an hour each month to someone building their way.
  5. I will improve: fix one thing near me, with evidence, not anger.
  6. I will progress: measure my year honestly, and begin the next one higher.
  7. I will stand against no one: caste, faith, region and party stay outside the work.
