The Forts

He held hundreds of forts. In 2025 UNESCO inscribed twelve of them as the Maratha Military Landscapes of India. Here are twenty-three of the most important, each with a source you can check.

// Fort stewards

Adopt a fort. Keep its record.

Forts are cared for by the people who climb them. A Fort Steward visits one fort a few times a year, photographs its condition, reports damage to the responsible archaeology office, and helps others visit with respect.

01

Choose one fort

One you can reach often. Depth beats breadth.

02

Record its condition

Gates, walls, water cisterns, litter, signage. Dated photographs, the same spots every visit.

03

Report through the right office

Forts are protected by the Archaeological Survey of India or the state's Directorate of Archaeology and Museums. We help you write to the right one.

04

Lead a visit

Take a school class or a group of first-timers. Tell them what happened there, from the sources.
