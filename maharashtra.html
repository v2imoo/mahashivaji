// Outreach

Every district needs three people first.

Not an office, not a chapter with a president. Three people who will carry the movement locally, without any party, fee or title.

I

District Steward

Keeps the district's file accurate: facts with sources, problems with evidence, possibilities with names attached.

II

Circle convenor

Gathers builders once a month: founders, students, professionals, farmer-entrepreneurs.

III

Civic Steward

Verifies local records for the Civic Lab before anything is published.
