Civic Lab

Improve the place you live in, with evidence and without anger. Citizens record, prioritise and act through the right channels. The Lab keeps the record and speaks for no party.

Pilot from May 2027

Three places first

One village, one town and one city ward, each with a trained Civic Steward.

Principle

Evidence before opinion

Every record is verified, weighed on the same measures, and published with its sources. Places and services are named; private individuals never are.

// Method

From grievance to priority to result.

01

Select the area

District, then village, town or ward.

02

Record the problem

Nature, location, photograph, people affected, and any prior complaint.

03

Verify

A Civic Steward confirms the particulars. Until then, the record is marked unverified.

04

Prioritise

Each verified problem is weighed on people affected, severity and strength of evidence.

05

Act

The responsible office, the official channel, and a prepared letter for citizens to send themselves.

06

Track

Replies and progress recorded on the same page, visible to the whole area.

// Before every election

Ten questions for every candidate.

The same questions, for every candidate, at every level of government. The Foundation publishes the questions, never a verdict on the answers.

  1. Which services, and what budget, does this office actually control?
  2. What are the three most pressing problems in this area?
  3. By what measures, and by when, will you show progress?
  4. Which commitment from the last term was fulfilled, and where is the record?
  5. How often will you account to residents for expenditure?
  6. How may a resident meet you, and how quickly will your office reply?
  7. Will you attend gram sabha or ward meetings regularly?
  8. What will you do in your first hundred days?
  9. What is your commitment to residents who did not vote for you?
  10. Where may we read your election affidavit?
