// The founding sequence

Eight steps to a sound enterprise.

Each step names the official route. Regulations change, so confirm on the source before acting.

01

Without charge

Set out the plan on one page

What you offer, to whom, at what price and at what cost. Have a mentor challenge it.

02

Bank charges vary

Separate the money

A dedicated bank account for the business, opened on your PAN, from the first rupee.

03

Without charge

Register on Udyam

Paperless registration for small enterprises, using Aadhaar and PAN. No agent is ever required.

04

Where applicable

Determine whether GST applies

Required above the turnover threshold and for most online selling. Registration is free; consult a chartered accountant if unsure.

05

Where applicable

Notify the Labour Department

Shops and offices: smaller establishments file an online intimation; larger ones register.

06

Nominal fee

Food business? FSSAI registration

Basic registration applies even to home kitchens.

07

Without charge

Innovative venture? Startup India recognition

Valuable where the business is new in its approach and built to scale.

08

Fee applies

Protect the name at IP India

A trademark becomes worthwhile once the name carries value.

// Capital pathways

Finance that fits the stage.

Capital should follow traction. Each route suits a different moment in a venture's life.

I

Self-funded

Revenue from first customers is the cheapest capital there is. Start small and prove demand.

→II

Credit

Collateral-free bank credit and margin-money support for micro and small enterprises.

→III

Incubation

State and national programmes offering incubation, pilots and grant support to innovative ventures.

→IV

Investors

Angel and venture capital for ventures built to scale, through introductions on the Network.

// Integrity

Recognising a fraudulent offer.

  • Udyam registration has no fee. A site that charges is not the government.
  • No genuine loan is sanctioned against a payment made over WhatsApp.
  • Transact only on sites ending in .gov.in, or on your bank's own site.
  • Never disclose a one-time password to a caller.

// Counsel

Put a question to a mentor.

Verified mentors answer as their time allows. Selected questions may be published without attribution, so that others benefit.

Responses are general guidance, not professional advice.

How to ask

  1. Write to us with the subject Question for a mentor.
  2. Tell us your town, your stage (Spark, Launch, Grow or Scale) and the subject.
  3. Describe what you do, what you have tried, and the decision in front of you.
