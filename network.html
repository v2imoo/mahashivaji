// How it works

Connection, by design.

Profiles are built around expertise and intent, not status. Every mentor is verified before appearing.

01

Profile by skill

Members describe what they know, what they can help with and what they are looking for.

02

Verification

Mentors' credentials and track records are checked before their profile goes live.

03

Matching

Questions and needs are matched to people with relevant, proven experience.

04

Office hours

Mentors keep regular, time-boxed hours. One conversation can change a decision.

05

Circles

Small groups by place, profession or stage meet to learn from one another.

06

Follow-through

Introductions are tracked, so that help actually arrives and outcomes are recorded.

// Opening sequence

Trust first, then scale.

The Network opens in three stages, each only once the one before is working well.

I

Mentor RegisterAdmissions open

Verified professionals and practitioners with public profiles, answering questions and keeping office hours.

II

MembersFrom August 2027

Any adult with a verified phone number may build a profile, follow mentors and write to them.

III

CirclesThereafter

Circles by locality and profession, mentoring matches, and openings for employment and apprenticeship.

// Who belongs

Builders of every kind.

FoundersFirst-time and experienced, from home kitchens to technology firms.
ProfessionalsChartered accountants, advocates, engineers, doctors, designers, technologists.
Students & graduatesSeeking direction, internships, apprenticeships and a second qualification.
The diasporaMarathi professionals across India and the world, giving an hour from wherever they are.
CraftspeopleMasters of trades, building reputations for quality and integrity.
Farmer-entrepreneursProducer companies and growers moving into processing and markets.
Public-minded citizensPeople improving their villages, towns and wards with evidence.
InstitutionsColleges, firms and associations that lend mentors and opportunities.

// Principles

What the Network will never ask, or allow.

Never asked

  • Caste
  • Religion
  • Political affiliation
  • Income

Never allowed

  • Selling, fees or solicitation of money
  • Party campaigning of any kind
  • Hostility towards any community, region or language
  • Accounts for those under eighteen
